Privacy and cookies

Last updated 20 November 2026

Who is responsible for your information

Nursing Citizen Development CIC is the controller of the personal information described here. We are a community interest company registered in England and Wales, company no. 17324367. Registered office: Flat 2 Fenton House, Hickman Street, Newcastle, ST5 2AR. Contact: info@nursingcitizendevelopment.com.

What we collect and why

When you download the handbook. The download is free and we don't ask for your name, email or any other details. The file is stored on Google Drive, which may log the download in the usual way.

Emails about new resources. If you previously asked us to email you about new NCD resources, we only do so with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by replying to any email.

When you email us. We use your message and email address to reply. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

When you visit this website. Our host, GitHub Pages, records technical information such as your IP address in its server logs to keep the site secure and running. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics also collects information about your visit (see below).

Cookies

We use one optional type of cookie: Google Analytics cookies (named _ga and _ga_NNVRRR8ZN8), which last up to two years. They tell us how many people visit, which pages they read, roughly where they are (country or city, not your exact location), what device they use, which website or post sent them, and whether they click the download button. We use this to improve the page and see which posts help people find it. Google Signals and advertising features are switched off.

We only set these cookies if you click "Accept analytics cookies". If you reject them, or make no choice, Google Analytics does not load at all. We remember your choice in your browser's local storage, which is strictly necessary and is not shared with anyone.

The free sample PDF downloads directly from this website and collects no information.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers, who process information for us under contract:

  • Google (Google Workspace): sending emails and storing the download file; and, only if you accept, Google Analytics.
  • GitHub: hosting this website.

We never sell your information. We may disclose it if the law requires us to.

Transfers outside the UK

Google and GitHub may process information in the United States and other countries. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the UK's adequacy regulations for the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Marketing preferences: until you ask us to stop.
  • Emails you send us: up to two years after the matter is closed.
  • Google Analytics data: 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, ask for it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. Email info@nursingcitizendevelopment.com and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.

Changes

We will update this notice if how we use information changes, and show the date at the top.
